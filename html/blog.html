If I cared about someone, and I do, and I wanted them to want by company or companionship, I would be kind to them. I don't understand the rudeness that I deal with sometimes. The nastiness. If she needs help there is bitching and whining, not an ask for help, not a kind plea. A cry until I offer help. But my help is also never good enough. She's impatient, but is constantly belittling me and claiming I'm impatient, constantly urging me to just wait, not to get impatient, when all I'm doing there is literally sitting and waiting on her. She's so afraid I'll get impatient and leave that she puts it on me. I hate that. Because that's just a way to take control and make it so the other person can't take something from you. Where, if you were just a kind likable person, you wouldn't have to constantly be fearful that I'm trying to escape away from you.
